## 原文

> At that time she received a samizdat copy of In Exile from the Land of Snows by John F. Avedon.

## 现译

>  當時她接到一本地下出版社發行、約翰・阿維登（John F. Avedon）寫的《從雪國出亡》（In Exile from the Land of Snows）。
